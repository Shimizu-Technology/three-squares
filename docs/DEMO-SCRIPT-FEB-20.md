# Three Squares Demo Script — Feb 20, 2026

**Meeting:** Follow-up with Marie Guerrero (B&G Pacific)
**Goal:** Show a production-ready ordering platform and close the engagement
**Duration:** 30-45 min demo, then discussion

---

## Opening (2 min)

"Since our last meeting, we've built a complete ordering platform tailored for Three Squares. Let me walk you through what your customers and staff will see."

**Key stat to mention:** 110 menu items across 16 categories, 2 locations already configured.

---

## Part 1: Customer Experience (10 min)

### 1a. Homepage
- **Show:** Hero section with "Good Food. Good Mood." branding
- **Point out:** Category cards (Daily Menu, Catering, Locations), featured products
- **Talking point:** "This is what customers see when they visit threesquaresguam.com"

### 1b. Menu Browsing
- Navigate to **Products** page
- **Show:** All 16 collections (Breakfast, Main Dishes, Sides, Desserts, Cookie Gift Boxes, Catering Bentos, etc.)
- Click into a collection → show items with images, prices
- Click a product → product detail page with variants, add to cart
- **Talking point:** "Menu is managed by your team — no developer needed to update"

### 1c. Latte Stone Cookies (Shipping)
- Navigate to Cookie Gift Boxes collection
- **Show:** Multiple box sizes (16pc, 30pc, etc.) with shipping support
- **Point out:** "These can ship anywhere in the US — integrated with EasyPost for real-time shipping rates"
- **Talking point:** "Perfect for the online business Marie mentioned"

### 1d. Cart & Checkout
- Add a few items to cart
- Open cart drawer → show quantities, modifications
- Go to checkout:
  - **Pickup option** → choose location (Main or Donki)
  - **Shipping option** → address form, real-time shipping rates
  - **Payment** → Stripe integration (show test mode)
- **Talking point:** "Customers can pay online and just pick up. No more phone tag."

### 1e. Catering
- Navigate to /catering
- **Show:** Catering inquiry form
- **Point out:** "Customers fill out event details, you get notified, respond with a quote"
- **Talking point:** "This replaces the email/phone back-and-forth for catering requests"

---

## Part 2: Admin / Staff Dashboard (15 min)

### 2a. Dashboard Overview
- Log in as admin → /admin
- **Show:** Dashboard with stats (orders, revenue, product counts)
- **Talking point:** "At a glance, you see what's happening across both locations"

### 2b. Order Management
- Go to **Orders** page
- **Show:** Order list with filters (status, location, date)
- Click an order → detail view with timeline
- **Show:** Status updates (Pending → Confirmed → Preparing → Ready → Completed)
- **Show:** Pickup Queue — dedicated view for kitchen staff
- **Point out:** Shipping Queue for cookie orders
- **Talking point:** "Kitchen staff see exactly what to make. No miscommunication."

### 2c. POS Mode (⭐ Key Feature)
- Click **POS** in sidebar
- **Show:** Full point-of-sale interface
  - Browse menu by category
  - Add items to order
  - Select location
  - Accept **cash** or **card** payment
  - Stripe Terminal S700 integration for card reader
- **Talking point:** "Your staff can take orders right at the counter — same system, no separate POS hardware subscription"
- **Compare:** "Replaces Square/Toast monthly fees"

### 2d. Menu Management
- Go to **Products** → show the full product list
- Click **Edit** on a product → change price, description, image
- **Show:** Collections management (reorder, hide, create new)
- **Show:** Variant system (sizes, options)
- **Show:** Inventory tracking
- **Talking point:** "Your team manages the menu. Add daily specials in seconds."

### 2e. Catering Management
- Go to **Catering** in admin
- **Show:** Incoming inquiries with event details
- **Show:** Quote and status workflow
- **Talking point:** "Track every catering request from inquiry to completion"

### 2f. Locations
- Go to **Locations** in admin
- **Show:** Two locations configured (Main + Donki)
- **Point out:** Menu items can be location-specific
- **Talking point:** "If you open more locations, just add them here"

### 2g. Settings
- Show **Settings** page
- **Point out:** Store name, contact info, payment mode, notification settings
- **Talking point:** "You control everything from one place"
