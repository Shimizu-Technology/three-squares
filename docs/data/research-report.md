## Executive Summary

Three Squares Restaurant is a **locally owned comfort food restaurant** in Tamuning, operated by **B&G Pacific LLC**, a **woman-owned, HUBZone certified** food services company. The business has two distinct but connected operations: a restaurant (Three Squares) and an institutional/commercial catering arm (B&G Pacific). They've expanded to include a second location at Don Quijote (Donki). Current ordering is primarily phone/email-based for catering - significant opportunity for digital ordering and catering management.

## Current Pain Points & Opportunities

### Pain Points (Inferred from Research)

**For Catering:**
1. **Manual order intake** - Phone/email for all catering requests
2. **No online menu with real-time pricing** - PDF menus, static
3. **Quote management** - Likely handled via email/phone
4. **No customer portal** - Repeat customers can't easily reorder
5. **Scaling challenges** - $3M business with manual processes

**For Restaurant:**
1. **No online ordering** - Walk-in and phone only
2. **No loyalty program visible**
3. **Wait times** when busy (mentioned in reviews)
4. **Second location coordination** - Donki location needs integration

### Opportunities for Ordering/Catering Software

**High-Value Features:**

1. **Catering Order Portal**
   - Online menu with pricing
   - Customizable platter builder
   - Event date/time scheduling
   - Lead time enforcement (2-3 days for specialty items)
   - Quote generation and approval
   - Deposit/payment processing

2. **Bulk Bento Ordering**
   - Corporate accounts
   - Recurring order setup
   - Multiple delivery locations
   - Headcount-based ordering

3. **Restaurant Online Ordering**
   - Pickup orders
   - Integration with both locations
   - Real-time menu availability

4. **Customer Management**
   - CRM for catering clients
   - Order history
   - Preference tracking
   - Automated reorder reminders

5. **Kitchen Management**
   - Order consolidation
   - Production planning for large events
   - Inventory integration

### Why They'd Be a Great Fit
- **Scale** - $3M revenue means budget for proper solution
- **Pain point clarity** - Manual catering process at scale is painful
- **Tech-savvy owners** - Already use WhatsApp for orders
- **Growth trajectory** - Second location, expanding
- **HUBZone certification** - May have government catering contracts requiring documentation
